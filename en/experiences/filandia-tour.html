Filandia Tour

Filandia is one of the best-preserved towns in the Coffee Cultural Landscape: colourful balconies, bahareque (wattle-and-daub) architecture and a basket-weaving tradition that is still alive.

  • Duration4 h
  • SchedulePickup: 9:00 AM or 2:00 PM
  • WhereFilandia, Quindío
  • FormatPrivate
  • LunchNo lunch

Good to know

  • Ideal for ages 4 to 80.

🏘️ Filandia: colours and basket weaving!

  • 👟 Comfortable walking shoes
  • 🧥 A jacket for the rain (Filandia is cool)
  • 💵 Cash for local crafts
  • 💧 Reusable bottle (we give you water on the tour)
