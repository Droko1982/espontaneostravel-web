Bee Tour

Wearing a beekeeper's suit, step into the apiary and learn from a local family how artisanal honey is harvested and why bees are key to coffee and the region's other crops.

Good to know

  • Bring insect repellent, comfortable long-sleeved clothes and hiking boots.
  • Don't wear perfume or scented creams.
  • When booking, send each participant's size for the beekeeping suit.
  • Ideal for ages 10 to 60.
  • Not suitable for people allergic to insect or bee stings.

🐝 Step into the sweet world of bees!

  • 📏 When booking, tell us each person's clothing size: we'll lend you a beekeeper suit
  • 👖 Long trousers and 👟 closed-toe shoes (boots if you have them)
  • 🚫 No perfumes, scented creams or very bright colours: they attract bees
  • ⚠️ Let us know if you're allergic to bee stings
  • 🧢 Hat or cap, sunscreen and insect repellent
  • 💧 Reusable bottle (we give you water on the tour)
  • 🚯 Don't leave any litter: leave the place as you found it
