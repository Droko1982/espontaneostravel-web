Good to know

  • Wear comfortable clothes, lace-up shoes and sunglasses, and bring a warm layer.
  • Suitable for people with reduced mobility.
  • The flight lasts 15 to 20 minutes, depending on the weather.
  • Ideal for ages 4 to 65.
  • Not suitable for people who have had recent surgery, pregnant women or anyone under the influence of alcohol or other substances.
