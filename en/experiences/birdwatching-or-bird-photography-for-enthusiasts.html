Birdwatching and bird photography for enthusiasts

Colombia has more bird species than any other country on the planet, and the Coffee Region is one of its best destinations. With an expert guide and binoculars, even if you have never done it before, you get to see and photograph species such as the Andean Motmot, the Emerald Toucanet and tanagers.

  • Duration2 h
  • ScheduleStart between 5:00 AM and 6:00 AM
  • WhereQuindío, Risaralda, Caldas, Northern Valle del Cauca
  • FormatPrivate
