Traditional city tour of Armenia

Armenia, the “Miracle City”, was rebuilt after the 1999 earthquake. The tour includes the Quimbaya Gold Museum, designed by the architect Rogelio Salmona, with one of the most important collections of pre-Columbian goldwork in the country.
