Birds · Alto del Campanario (páramo)

Páramo and high Andean forest birds between 3,400 and 4,600 m above sea level: mixed flocks, tapaculos, antpittas and high-altitude tanagers in a landscape of mist and frailejón plants. For birders in good physical condition.

Good to know

  • Wear long-sleeved, waterproof clothes in greens, browns or olive (no white or bright colours) and hiking boots.
  • Bring thermal clothing, gloves and a warm hat, and plenty of water.
  • If you have binoculars or a camera, bring them.
  • Not suitable for people with a history of heart or respiratory conditions.
  • Minimum 2 people; large groups are split up.
