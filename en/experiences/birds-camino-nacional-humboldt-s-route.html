Birds · Camino Nacional (Humboldt's route)

The old trail travelled by Humboldt and Bonpland in 1801, through cloud forest at 3,370 m above sea level. It is one of the best places in the country to see the Yellow-eared Parrot, which nests in the wax palm and was on the brink of extinction.

Good to know

  • Wear long-sleeved, waterproof clothes in greens, browns or olive (no white or bright colours) and hiking boots.
  • If you have binoculars or a camera, bring them.
  • Bring insect repellent, water, a warm layer and thermal clothing: it is cold early in the morning.
  • Minimum 2 people; large groups are split up.
