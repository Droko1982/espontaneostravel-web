Deluxe horse ride in the cordillera

A premium ride on Arabian and Spanish horses through the cordillera, with a training demonstration, the planting of a native tree, a luxury picnic and a return by Willys with a mountain-style lunch.

  • SchedulePickup at 9:00 AM
  • WhereQuindío
  • FormatPrivate

What's included

  • Transport
  • Horse-handling briefing
  • Accompanied by cowboys
  • Protective gear (helmet)
  • Luxury picnic and country-style lunch
  • Fruit tasting and hydration
  • Planting activity
  • Willys jeep experience
  • Digital photos

Not included

  • Unrelated expenses are not included.

Good to know

  • Bring insect repellent, comfortable clothes and a hat or cap.
  • Ideal for ages 12 to 60.
  • Not suitable for pregnant women or people with back problems.
  • Certified under the NTS AV 014 standard (horse riding in adventure tourism) and for good equine practices.

🐎 A deluxe ride in the cordillera!

  • 👖 Long trousers
  • 👟 Boots or closed-toe shoes
  • 🤠 Hat or cap and sunscreen
  • 🧴 Insect repellent
  • 🌧️ It may rain: we'll lend you a reusable poncho
  • ⚠️ Let us know your approximate weight and your experience with horses
  • 🪖 We'll give you a helmet: wear it for the whole ride
