Agency FAQ

What kind of companies do you work with?

Travel agencies, tour operators, wholesalers and event and incentive organisers, in Colombia and abroad.

How do I get net rates?

Request access to the agency portal. Once approved, you'll see current net rates by group size, technical sheets and conditions.

Do you operate white label?

Yes. We operate under your agency's brand, and product sheets and sales kits can be downloaded without our logo.

Which areas do you cover?

Quindío, Risaralda, Caldas and northern Valle del Cauca: Salento and the Cocora Valley, Filandia, Armenia, Pereira, Manizales and the mountain towns of the Cordillera.

Which languages do you work in?

Guides in Spanish and English; other languages on request. Product sheets and the sales kit are available in Spanish, English, French, Italian and Portuguese.

Do you handle groups and incentives?

Yes. We design programmes for groups, incentive and corporate travel, with vehicles for up to 30 passengers.

Shall we work together?

Request portal access or message us and we'll send you the 2027 portfolio.
