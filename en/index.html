About us

Over 15 years connecting you with coffee country

We are a tour operator in the Coffee Cultural Landscape specialising in rural, nature, community and wellness experiences. We work with farming families, coffee growers, artisans and local guides to connect you authentically and responsibly with the region's nature and culture.

Sustainability

Tourism that leaves a positive mark

We know that tourism transforms destinations. That's why we work hand in hand with communities and the land: farming families, artisans and local guides are part of what we offer, and we support them with advice so their experiences keep their authenticity and quality.

🌿 Environmental

We care for our surroundings

  • Water for every traveller on all tours, with no single-use plastics
  • Reusable rain ponchos
  • Waste separation and energy saving
  • Tree planting to offset the carbon footprint, with a certificate for agencies

  • We buy local crafts and food
  • We advise small producers on designing their experiences
  • We train guides and suppliers
  • We promote the care of cultural heritage
💚 Economic

FAQ

Your questions answered

Answers from Jenny, our assistant. Anything else? Message us.

Who is Espontáneos Travel?
Hi! We're Espontáneos Travel, a tour operator in the Coffee Cultural Landscape with more than 15 years of experience. We specialise in rural, nature, community and wellness experiences. Our purpose is to connect you authentically and responsibly with the nature and culture of our region. 🌿☕
How do I book and what do I need?
It's very easy! To confirm your booking we need the full names and ID documents of the passengers, along with proof of payment of the deposit. Once we receive them, we'll send you the formal confirmation of your trip. 🎒
How much do the experiences cost?
The price depends on the number of people and where you're staying. Tell me how many people and the date, and I'll send you a quote.
Do I pay everything upfront? Which payment methods do you accept?
To book, you pay a 50% deposit. Your quote shows the balance due and the payment deadlines. Payment methods, in the name of Espontáneos Travel SAS (NIT 902.056.513): 🏦 Bancolombia savings: 756-000062-16 📱 Nequi or Daviplata: +57 317 645 6723 💳 Debit or credit card: secure Wompi payment button 💵 You can pay the balance in Colombian pesos, US dollars or euros. 📩 Send us the receipt on WhatsApp. ⚠️ We only use these methods. If anyone asks you to pay into another account, check with us first.
Do you offer airport transfers?
We provide transfers from El Edén (Armenia) and Matecaña (Pereira) airports to hotels in Quindío and Risaralda, and between Armenia, Pereira and Manizales. The journey takes approx. 1 h.
What should I bring?
To make the most of the Coffee Cultural Landscape, bring very comfortable clothes, sunscreen and insect repellent, stay well hydrated and keep your ID document handy. ☀️💧
What's the weather like?
Mild climate all year round; it can rain in any season. It gets cold in Cocora, in the páramo and among the wax palms.
When is high season?
High season 2026: Holy Week (28 Mar–5 Apr), school break (3–12 Oct) and the year-end holidays (19 Dec 2026–11 Jan 2027). Prices and availability vary.
Is it suitable for children and older adults?
Of course! For children, some walks require adult supervision and basic fitness. For older adults, we recommend the options that best suit their mobility. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Child rate for ages 3 to 10; children under 3 pay a minimum rate without meals. At Parque del Café, the child rate applies to heights between 91 and 124 cm.
Do you have accessible, barrier-free options?
Yes! We have adapted options and special vehicles so everyone can enjoy the region without barriers. We also have a guide for blind travellers. Ask us and we'll give you personalised advice. ♿
Which languages do the guides speak?
Guides in Spanish, English, French, Italian and Portuguese (guiding in a language other than Spanish costs extra). On the Shared Tour, the English-speaking guide carries a supplement depending on group size.
What if I cancel?
We understand that plans change. Cancellation terms depend on how far in advance you let us know. Tell me which tour you booked and we'll review your case with an advisor.
Are you a sustainable company?
We work with farming families, artisans and local guides, with fair pay. We give you water on every tour and a reusable rain poncho (zero single-use plastics), we separate waste and we plant trees to offset our carbon footprint. We reject the sexual exploitation of children and adolescents (ESCNNA).
