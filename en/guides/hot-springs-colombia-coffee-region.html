Hot springs in the Coffee Region

The Coffee Region's hot springs are fed by the volcanic activity of the Central Cordillera and emerge at around 70 °C. Surrounded by waterfalls and forest, they are the best way to rest after your hikes.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

Santa Rosa de Cabal and San Vicente

In Santa Rosa de Cabal, in Risaralda, you'll find the Santa Rosa and San Vicente hot springs, in the mountains near the town.

The experience includes transport, hydration and entrance to the hot springs reserve of your choice.

Hot springs: Santa Rosa, San Vicente or El Otoño4 hRelax in hot springs (Santa Rosa, San Vicente, El Otoño or Tierra Viva).

El Otoño, next to Manizales

The El Otoño hot springs are in Villamaría, next to Manizales, in Caldas: a good option if you are staying in that area.

How to get there

Pereira is the shortest journey: 15 km to Santa Rosa de Cabal, about 25 min. From Armenia it is 61 km and from Manizales 38 km.

What to pack

A swimsuit, a towel, insect repellent and comfortable clothes. Not recommended for people with a history of heart conditions.

It is busier at weekends and in high season: check opening times before you go.

How to combine them

The hot springs are the perfect end to a day of hiking in the Cocora Valley or birdwatching at Otún Quimbaya, and a good alternative to Parque del Café on a short trip.

FAQ

How hot is the water?

It emerges at around 70 °C, due to the volcanic activity of the Central Cordillera.

Which hot springs are closest to Pereira?

Santa Rosa de Cabal and San Vicente: the town of Santa Rosa de Cabal is 15 km from Pereira.

Can children go?

Yes, the experience is ideal for ages 6 to 80.
