Tours from Armenia

Armenia, the “Miracle City”, is the heart of Quindío: from here you can reach Salento, Filandia and the theme parks in an hour or less, and El Edén airport is 17 km away.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

In the city

The traditional city tour includes the Museo del Oro Quimbaya (Quimbaya Gold Museum), designed by Rogelio Salmona, with one of the most important collections of pre-Columbian goldwork in the country. The cuyabro art city tour takes in murals, graffiti and barranquismo, and ends with a tasting of single-origin coffees.

Traditional city tour of Armenia4 hExplore Quindío's capital: history, parks and local culture. City tour: Cuyabro art and single-origin coffee4 hLocal art and origin coffee on an urban tour with authentic flavour.

Cocora Valley, Salento and Filandia

Salento is 21 km away (about 40 min) and Filandia 30 km. The shared tour leaves Armenia daily, and there is also a private tour and a full day covering all three destinations.

Theme parks

Parque del Café is in Montenegro, 12 km away; PANACA is in Quimbaya, 22 km away; and RECUCA and the Botanical Garden are in Calarcá, 6 km away.

Parque del Café7 hThe Coffee Region's flagship theme park: culture, rides and coffee. PANACA7 hThe largest agricultural nature park: animals and live shows. RECUCA Coffee Culture Tour4 hExperience coffee culture interactively: dress up and harvest coffee.

Coffee, countryside and adventure

Near Armenia there are coffee farms, cacao, fruit and plantain tours, canopy zip lines about 15 minutes from the city, and paragliding or paratrike flights over Quindío.

Coffee farm visit4 hFrom seed to cup on a traditional farm, with an origin coffee tasting. ☕ Canopy adventure in nature3 hZip wires over the forest canopy, near Armenia. Paragliding1 hSoar by paraglider and admire the Coffee Cultural Landscape from the sky.

Mountain towns of the Cordillera

The full day through Córdoba, Pijao and Buenavista shows you the lesser-known Quindío; Pijao is 36 km from Armenia.

Full Day Mountain Towns of the Cordillera: Córdoba, Pijao & Buenavista8 hThree mountain towns and an unforgettable coffee viewpoint.

FAQ

What is there to do near Armenia?

The Cocora Valley and Salento, Filandia, Parque del Café, PANACA, RECUCA, coffee farms, canopy zip lines, paragliding and the city tour with the Museo del Oro Quimbaya.

Where does the shared tour to Cocora leave from?

From Armenia, with meeting points at the Plaza de Bolívar (8:00 a.m.) and Parque de la Vida (8:10 a.m.).

How far is the airport?

El Edén (AXM) is 17 km from Armenia, about 30 min.
