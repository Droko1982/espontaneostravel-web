Theme parks in the Coffee Region

The Coffee Region is one of Colombia's best destinations for family travel: parks devoted to coffee, the countryside, nature and the history of the region, all close to one another.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

Parque del Café

In Montenegro, 12 km from Armenia, this is the Coffee Region's most emblematic theme park: coffee culture told through shows, the coffee trail, a funicular, a cable car and fairground rides.

Parque del Café7 hThe Coffee Region's flagship theme park: culture, rides and coffee.

PANACA

In Quimbaya, 22 km from Armenia, this is the park of farming culture, built on the idea that “without the countryside, there is no city”: more than 2.5 km of stations with farm animals and shows featuring horses, dogs and pigs.

PANACA7 hThe largest agricultural nature park: animals and live shows.

RECUCA and the Botanical Garden

In Calarcá, 6 km from Armenia. At RECUCA you dress up as a coffee picker and live the coffee cycle; the Quindío Botanical Garden has the National Palm Collection, the largest palm collection in Colombia, and a butterfly house.

RECUCA Coffee Culture Tour4 hExperience coffee culture interactively: dress up and harvest coffee. Quindío Botanical Garden & Butterfly House3 hButterfly house and forest: Quindío's biodiversity in a living garden.

Bioparque Ukumarí

Pereira is home to one of the largest bioparks in Latin America, dedicated to conservation: spectacled bears, pumas, Andean birds and an African savannah with giraffes, lions and rhinos.

Bioparque Ukumarí4 hOne of Latin America's most modern bio-parks, with wildlife from several continents.

Parque Los Arrieros and Laberinto Mil Caminos

Parque Los Arrieros, in Montenegro, pays tribute to arriería (mule driving), the trade that built the region, with 8 artistic performances and 14 interactive activities. Laberinto Mil Caminos, in Quimbaya, has more than 3 km of paths among 24,000 shrubs.

☕ Parque Los Arrieros6 hMuleteer tradition, humour and 'paisa' culture in a unique theme park. Laberinto Mil Caminos3 hFamily fun in a great green maze of a thousand paths.

Which one should I choose?

For coffee culture with fairground rides, Parque del Café; for the countryside and animals, PANACA; to experience the coffee process first-hand, RECUCA; for wildlife, Ukumarí. If you have two days, combine PANACA and Parque del Café, as in our Family & Theme Parks itinerary.

FAQ

Which is the best park for children?

It depends on their age and interests: Parque del Café has fairground rides; PANACA, farm animals and shows; and Ukumarí, Andean and African wildlife. They are all ideal for families.

Do young children pay?

At PANACA, RECUCA and Parque Los Arrieros, children under 90 cm go free. At Parque del Café, the rate depends on height.

Do the experiences include transport?

Yes. They include transport and park entry (the Pasaporte Múltiple at Parque del Café and the Pasaporte Terra at PANACA).
