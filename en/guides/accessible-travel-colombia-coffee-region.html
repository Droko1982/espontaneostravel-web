Accessible travel in the Coffee Region

We want everyone to enjoy the Coffee Cultural Landscape. That's why we are certified in Accessible Tourism (CIDCA – ProColombia 2025) and have special vehicles, adapted routes and personalised advice for every traveller.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

How we do it

We have adapted options and special vehicles, a guide for blind travellers, and flexible pacing and stops.

Before you book, we give you personalised advice so you can choose the experiences that best suit each person.

Experiences suitable for people with reduced mobility

The paratrike, a motorised paraglider, is suitable for people with reduced mobility: a 15- to 20-minute flight with a 360° view over Quindío and Valle del Cauca.

At the bird garden in Circasia you watch and photograph birds from a covered terrace, with no physical effort and sheltered from sun and rain.

Coffee at your own pace

The coffee farm visit adapts to each group's pace, and the specialty coffee cupping can be done at your hotel or wherever you are.

Barrier-Free Coffee Cultural Landscape

Our Barrier-Free Coffee Cultural Landscape itinerary suggests two days: a coffee farm visit and a day at Parque del Café. We adapt it to each group.

Before you travel

Write to us in advance and tell us what each person needs (mobility, vision, pace, companions) so we can book the right vehicle and confirm every stop.

FAQ

Do you have adapted vehicles?

Yes. We have special vehicles and adapted routes; when you book, we confirm the vehicle according to the group's needs.

Are there experiences for people with reduced mobility?

Yes. The paratrike and the bird garden in Circasia are suitable for people with reduced mobility, and we can advise you on adapting other experiences.

Do you have a guide for blind travellers?

Yes, we have a guide for blind travellers. Write to us and we'll arrange the experience.

What certification do you have?

We are certified in Accessible Tourism (CIDCA – ProColombia 2025).

Want to live this experience?

Message us with your date and group size. We welcome travellers and agencies from all over the world.
