Tours from Pereira

Pereira, the “Pearl of the Otún”, is the most dynamic city in the Coffee Region and a very convenient base: it has an airport (Matecaña) and is an hour and a half or less from most experiences.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

In the city

The city tour takes in the cathedral, the Plaza de Bolívar with Arenas Betancourt's Bolívar Desnudo (“Naked Bolívar”) and the César Gaviria Viaduct, rounded off with specialty coffee.

City tour of Pereira4 hDiscover the 'Pearl of the Otún': viewpoints, culture and coffee-city life.

Nature near Pereira

The Otún Quimbaya Flora and Fauna Sanctuary, less than an hour away, is a refuge for the Cauca guan and for howler monkeys. Barbas-Bremen, between Filandia and Pereira, is one of the last corridors of Andean forest in the region.

Nature reserves · Otún Quimbaya6 hThe protected forest of the Cauca Guan, near Pereira. Nature reserves · Barbas-Bremen3 hAndean forest and howler monkeys between Filandia and Pereira.

Hot springs and biopark

The town of Santa Rosa de Cabal, with its hot springs, is 15 km away (about 25 min), and Bioparque Ukumarí, with Andean and African wildlife, is in Pereira.

Hot springs: Santa Rosa, San Vicente or El Otoño4 hRelax in hot springs (Santa Rosa, San Vicente, El Otoño or Tierra Viva). Bioparque Ukumarí4 hOne of Latin America's most modern bio-parks, with wildlife from several continents.

Day trips to Quindío

From Pereira it is 37 km to Salento and 31 km to Filandia: the Cocora Valley and the coffee towns can be done in a day, with transport from your hotel.

Coffee

Coffee farm visits and specialty coffee cuppings are also available in Risaralda.

FAQ

What is there to do near Pereira?

The hot springs of Santa Rosa de Cabal, Otún Quimbaya, Bioparque Ukumarí, the city tour and coffee farms; and, as day trips, the Cocora Valley, Salento and Filandia.

Can I visit the Cocora Valley from Pereira?

Yes: Salento is 37 km from Pereira, about 1 h. We organise the tour with transport from your hotel.

Does Pereira have an airport?

Yes, Matecaña (PEI), a few minutes from the city centre.
