Things to do in Colombia's Coffee Region

Colombia's Coffee Cultural Landscape, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, brings together traditional towns, coffee farms, cloud forests and the wax palm, Colombia's national tree. This guide sums up the essentials by destination and links to the experiences to enjoy them.

Updated: 2026-10-22 · Espontáneos Travel

Salento and the Cocora Valley

The Cocora Valley is home to the wax palm, Colombia's national tree, and Salento is the oldest town in Quindío. Together they are the must-do of the Coffee Region.

FAQ

How many days do I need in the Coffee Region?

With 3 or 4 days you'll see the essentials: the Cocora Valley and Salento, a coffee farm, Filandia and hot springs or a theme park. With 5 to 7 days you can add birdwatching, the mountain towns and Manizales or Pereira.

Where should I stay in the Coffee Region?

Salento and Filandia are ideal to be close to the Cocora Valley; Armenia and Pereira have more services and airports; coffee farms offer the most peaceful experience.
