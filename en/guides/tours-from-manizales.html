Tours from Manizales

Manizales, built on mountainsides and overlooking the Nevado del Ruiz, is the gateway to Caldas: world-class birding, hot springs and farms, 51 km from Pereira.

Updated: 2026-10-26 · Espontáneos Travel

In the city

The city tour takes you to one of the tallest cathedrals in Latin America, which you climb via the Polish Corridor, and includes the cable car, the historic centre and coffee at a viewpoint.

City tour of Manizales4 hCathedral, cable car and mountain architecture of this welcoming city.

Birdwatching at Río Blanco

Minutes from Manizales, the Río Blanco Reserve protects the city's water and is one of Colombia's best-known birding destinations, famous for its antpitta feeders.

Nature reserves · Río Blanco4 hCloud forest and antpitta feeders next to Manizales.

Hot springs

The El Otoño hot springs are next to Manizales, in Villamaría, and the town of Santa Rosa de Cabal, with its hot springs, is 38 km away.

Hot springs: Santa Rosa, San Vicente or El Otoño4 hRelax in hot springs (Santa Rosa, San Vicente, El Otoño or Tierra Viva).

Orchids and fruit

La Romelia, a farm near Manizales, is devoted to orchids, bonsai and fruit trees, with a traditional country lunch included.

☕ Visit to La Romelia · orchids and exotic fruits4 hOrchids, bonsai and exotic fruits on a farm near Manizales.

Day trips to the rest of the Coffee Region

From Manizales it is 51 km to Pereira and 86 km to Salento: you can see the Cocora Valley in a day if you set off early.

Cocora Valley & Salento Tour7 hThe world's tallest wax palms and the most colourful town in Quindío.

FAQ

What is there to do near Manizales?

The city tour with the cathedral, the Río Blanco Reserve for birdwatching, the El Otoño hot springs, La Romelia and coffee farms.

Can I visit the Cocora Valley from Manizales?

Yes, as a day trip: Salento is 86 km away, about 1 h 55 min. It's best to set off early.

Does Manizales have an airport?

Yes, La Nubia (MZL). You can also fly into Matecaña (PEI), in Pereira, 56 km away.
